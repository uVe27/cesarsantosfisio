Quick Health Myth-Buster

Common Myth

"I have a herniated disc on my MRI, so my spine is broken and damaged."

Scientific Reality

35% of people over 40 have herniated discs without feeling any pain (Varun S. et al., 2025). The image reflects normal age-related changes, like wrinkles on the skin, not a certificate of disability.

See reality →
Common Myth

"I need to find the perfect posture to sit or stand and thus avoid back pain."

Scientific Reality

There is no single or perfect posture. Science shows no causal link between static postures and pain. The body needs movement and variability; your best posture is the next one.

See reality →
Common Myth

"If a physical activity hurts, it means I am causing damage to my body tissues."

Scientific Reality

Persistent pain is a hypersensitive alarm system. Pain protects before actual damage occurs. Graded, adapted movement trains the brain to regulate sensitivity.
