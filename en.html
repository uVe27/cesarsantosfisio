The 4 pillars of integrative physiotherapy and PNI

Treating only the exact spot that hurts often leads to recurring flare-ups. Integrative Health and Clinical Psychoneuroimmunology (PNI) evaluate the full map: how you move, how your nervous system reacts, how you sleep, and which daily habits keep your pain alarm active.

Clinical approach and credentials →
01 · Manual Therapy

Hands-on manual therapy

Targeted manual techniques to calm acute pain and ease stiff joints or guarded muscles. They provide an opening window of relief so you can begin moving with confidence.

02 · Exercise & Pilates

Therapeutic exercise and Pilates

Properly dosed movement carries the strongest clinical backing. We design progressive routines to rebuild spinal strength, refine motor control, and restore trust in your body.

03 · Lifestyle & PNI

Clinical Psychoneuroimmunology (PNI)

Pain interacts continuously with the rest of your physiology. Poor sleep, sustained stress, or low-grade inflammation lowers your pain threshold. PNI helps adjust these drivers for lasting results.

04 · Pain Science

Pain neuroscience education

Understanding what happens in your body physically alters how intense symptoms feel. Clear explanations around MRIs and tissue resilience help your brain turn down unnecessary threat signals.

Frequently asked questions on health and pain

Direct answers grounded in biomedical literature for patients and clinicians.

Can I return to sports if I have a herniated disc or wear on my MRI?+

Yes. Research shows that herniated discs and disc dehydration appear in 35% of pain-free adults over 40. The spine adapts to progressive load, so a structured graded exercise plan lets you rebuild athletic capacity safely.

What is the difference between strict rest and active coping?+

Extended bed rest weakens supporting muscles, reduces disc nutrition, and heightens nervous system sensitivity. Active coping uses fixed time quotas (pacing) to retrain your nervous system so movement stops triggering a danger response.

How does Clinical Psychoneuroimmunology (PNI) fit into physiotherapy?+

PNI studies the communication between nervous, immune, and endocrine systems. In clinical care, it helps assess sleep quality, circadian rhythm, chronic stress, and low-grade inflammation that shape tissue healing.

Does learning pain neuroscience actually lower physical pain?+

Yes. Understanding pain physiology reduces fear-driven amygdala signaling and engages descending inhibitory pathways from the prefrontal cortex, releasing endogenous pain-modulating neurotransmitters.
